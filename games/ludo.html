📖 LUDO RULES

• Roll a 6 to bring a token out of base.
• Select a highlighted token after rolling.
• Tokens move one cell per step.
• Capture an opponent by landing on its token, except on safe squares.
• Roll a 6 or capture to earn another turn.
• Reach the finish with an exact roll.
• Bring all four tokens home to win.
• Three consecutive sixes forfeit the turn.
