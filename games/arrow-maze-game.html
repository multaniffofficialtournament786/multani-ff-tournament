🎮 Gameplay Rules
  • Arrow Escape: Arrowhead jis taraf point karega, arrow usi direction me bahar niklega.
  • Snake Motion: Turns wale arrows track follow karte huye smooth bahar nikalte hain.
  • Wall Bump: Blocked arrow tap karne par saamne takra kar wapas apni jagah aa jata hai.
  • Fast Combo Tap: Aage wale arrow ke peeche turant tap karne par bina life gawaye arrow queue ho jayega.
  • 3 Hearts: Har naye level par 3 Hearts reset milenge.
