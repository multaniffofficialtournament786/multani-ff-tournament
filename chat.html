CHAT RULES • Respect & Security Guide ▼
  1. Sabhi Players Ki Izzat Karein: Badtameezi, gaali-galoch ya toxic behaviour par foran ban milega.
  2. Room ID & Pass Kahi Bhi Share Karna Sakht Mana Hai: Custom room ki details kisi se bhi share na karein. Leak karne par tournament se permanent ban kiya jayega.
  3. Time Par Room Join Karein: ID aur Password aate hi waqt par apni slot join karein. Ghalat slot me baithne par kick kar diya jayega.
  4. Spamming Par Pabandi: Ek hi message baar baar bhejna ya lambe copy-paste karna mana hai.
  5. Private Details Safe Rakhein: Apna phone number, password, OTP ya koi private info chat me na bhejein.
  6. No Scamming & Fraud: ID buy/sell, fake diamonds ya fake giveaways par zero tolerance hai.
  7. No Promotion / Links: Kisi WhatsApp group, doosre tournament ya YouTube link ki ijazat nahi hai.
  8. No Third-Party Tools / Hacks: Kisi bhi illegal tool ya script ka use permanent ban karega.
  9. Siyasi / Mazhabi Behas Band: Chat sirf gaming ke liye hai; doosri behas se parhez karein.
  10. Admin Ka Faisla Final: Room rematch ya points issue par official admins ka faisla aakhri hoga.
  11. 12-Hour Message Life: Chat ka data fresh rakhne ke liye har message 12 ghante me gayab ho jata hai.
  12. Rules Violation: Violation par chat block aur team tournament se disqualify ho sakti hai.
